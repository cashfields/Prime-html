"step-1"
* wildcard numbers , no matter how large a number is it can only end with the digits 0 to 9.

Call these *wildcard , this is similar to the star (*) in regular expression.

"step-2"
There are eight number that make the first group of primes.

A number is a ps (prime suspect) until verified.

These are a subset of *wildcard ; call them *famXX.

*fam01 any number that ends with *1 and satisfies the formula:
*fam01 = (ps - 01)/30 is an integer.

*fam07 = (ps - 07)/30 is an integer.

*fam11 = (ps - 11)/30 is an integer.

*fam13 = (ps - 13)/30 is an integer.



*fam17 = (ps - 17)/30 is an integer.

*fam19 = (ps - 19)/30 is an integer.

*fam23 = (ps - 23)/30 is an integer.

*fam29 = (ps - 29)/30 is an integer.

"step-3"
This chart shows the difference between two planes. The numbers are separated by 10.

The one plane will have a name of R (right) for { 1 , 7 , 13 , 19 }.

The other plane will have a name of L (left) for { 11 , 17 , 23 , 29 }.

This is not an arbitrary name as will be show , later.

The ps (prime suspects) only end in four digits: { 1 , 3 , 7 , 9 }

"step-4"



There is a common value that internally separates the values: 6.

1 to 7 is 6 ; 7 to 13 is 6 ; 13 to 19 is 6.

11 to 17 is 6 ; 17 to 23 is 6 ; 23 to 29 is 6.

There are two values 10 and 6 that drive ps (prime suspects).

"step-5"



Listing the values from 0 to 30 as a group ; the first group.

The name of a group will be given the symbol (g) [group]

This is designated as (g)0 in the upper left corner.

Inside the chart are the formulas for R & L.

This chart is balanced , it starts with a 0 and ends with a 0.

The midpoint is 15 ; there are 31 things an odd number.

All the ps numbers for a given (g) are balanced by there location on the chart. They are equal distant from the ends and the midpoint.

01 + 29 =30

07 + 23=30

11 + 19=30

13 + 17=30

Since the next possible ps is 30 away from the prior for any *fam.

*fam01 is the series:
{ 1 , 31 , 61 , 91 , . . . }

*fam07 is the series:
{ 7 , 37 , 67 , 97 , . . . }

*fam11 is the series:
{ 11 , 41 , 71 , 101 , . . . }

This is the same for all *fam numbers.

"step-8"

In the chart are the eight formulas to solve each of *fam.

As (g) increases from 0 to 1 the formulas will simultaneous solve all equations , this is matrix math.

(g)n: *fam + (n)(3)(10) = ps

Just looking at one *fam ; *fam07.
(g)0 : 7 + (0)(3)(10) = 7
(g)1 : 7 + (1)(3)(10) = 37
(g)2 : 7 + (2)(3)(10) = 97
(g)3 : 7 + (3)(3)(10) = 127



"step-9"

The reverse calculation can be applied to any ps to find what (g) it belongs to.

Pick a number ending in 1 , 3 , 7 , 9.

Example: 143

There are two possible *fam associated , either *fam13 or *fam23.

Take 143 - 13 = 130 ; divide by 30 = 4.3333 - it is not an integer

Next 143 - 23 = 120 ; divide by 30 = 4 - it is an integer.

The answer is (g)4.

If neither equation solves to an integer the number is divisible by 3 and therefore not a ps.
